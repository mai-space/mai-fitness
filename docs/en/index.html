Fitness

Your training companion for gym and home: plans, automatic progression and progress, no account needed.

Coming soon to Google Play

What the app does

  • 63 exercises with instructions, beginner plans for gym and home
  • Log sets with rest timer, automatic progression of reps and weight
  • Progress with personal bests, forecast and 30 achievements
  • Reminders, “Next workout” widget and a Wear OS tile

Your data stays yours

  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your entries stay encrypted on your device.
  • Backup and export only when you want, as a file you keep yourself.

All details are in the privacy policy.
